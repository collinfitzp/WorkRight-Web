How it works

A short, straightforward process from first contact to a live website.

  1. 1

    Tell me about your business

    Complete a short intake form with information about your business, services, branding, and website needs.

  2. 2

    I build your website

    I create the website using the information and materials you provide.

  3. 3

    Review & launch

    You review the website and request any agreed-upon revisions before it goes live.

  4. 4

    I manage it

    If you choose ongoing management, I handle hosting-related upkeep and future basic updates according to the service agreement.
